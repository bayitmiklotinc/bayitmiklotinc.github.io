Our Approach

A Philosophy of
Integrated Care

We believe people heal best when every part of their life is cared for together.

Recovery and Healing with a Human Heart

We combine evidence-informed therapies, delivered by licensed partner clinicians, with genuine relationships, compassionate support, and personalized care.

  • Care is built around the person, not just the condition.
  • Community strengthens recovery.
  • Jewish values provide meaning and purpose.

Together, they create an environment where people are treated not as diagnoses, but as whole human beings.

Mental Health Recovery Housing Family Purpose Community

These are not separate challenges. They deserve an integrated response.

At Bayit Miklot, we do not ask people to move from one service to another.

We bring coordinated, evidence-informed clinical care, residential support, recovery, Jewish values, vocational development, and lifelong community around the individual.

Because lasting healing happens when every part of a person’s life is cared for together.

Guided by Timeless Principles

Everything we do is shaped by enduring Torah values.

Train each person according to their own way.

Every journey is unique. Every treatment plan should be too.

Do not stand idly by the blood of your neighbor.

Compassion calls us to action. We choose to show up.

Repair the world.

Every life restored strengthens families, communities, and future generations.

All of Israel is responsible for one another.

We are responsible for one another. No one should have to rebuild their life alone.

More Than a Program

We don’t measure success simply by completing treatment.

We measure it by restored relationships.

  • Renewed purpose.
  • Growing confidence.
  • Healthy independence.

And a community that continues long after formal treatment has ended.

Because recovery isn’t the destination.
It is the beginning of a life worth building.

“We don’t simply treat recovery. We help people rediscover who they were created to become.”
