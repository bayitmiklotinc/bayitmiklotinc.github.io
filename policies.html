Privacy Policy

Bayit Miklot respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through bayitmiklot.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email or call us, we receive your name, contact details, and anything you choose to share.
  • Donation information. Card donations are processed by a third-party payment processor, which collects your payment details and shares with us your name, email address, billing details, and the amount and date of your gift. We never see or store your full card number. Gifts through The Donors’ Fund or other donor-advised funds are handled by those organizations, which may share your name and gift details with us.
  • Automatic information. The Site uses the Google tag (Google Ads) to measure visits and the effectiveness of our outreach. Google may set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you visit, and the site that referred you. Google may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites.

How we limit advertising and tracking

  • Ad personalization is turned off. We do not use remarketing or build advertising audiences from visits to our program pages, and we do not use information about your health to target advertising.
  • For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, advertising cookies are off by default.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Health information

Please do not send detailed medical or mental health information by email or through the Site. Email is not a secure channel. If you are seeking help, share only your name and the best way to reach you, and we will arrange a confidential conversation.

How we use information

We use information:

  • to process donations and send receipts,
  • to respond to inquiries and requests for help,
  • to thank donors and share news about our work (you may opt out at any time),
  • to understand and improve the Site and our outreach, and
  • to meet legal, accounting, and tax obligations.

How we share information

We share personal information only:

  • with service providers that help us operate, such as our payment processor, Google (advertising measurement), and GitHub (website hosting),
  • with donor-advised fund sponsors, such as The Donors’ Fund, when you give through them,
  • when required by law or to protect the safety of any person, or
  • with your consent.

Your privacy choices

Global Privacy Control. If your browser sends the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out, and the Site turns off advertising cookies and ad-related data for your visit.

Do Not Track. There is no common standard for browser “Do Not Track” signals, so the Site does not respond to them. We honor Global Privacy Control as described above.

Cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can manage Google advertising settings at Google Ad Settings and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

Access, correction, and deletion. To unsubscribe from our emails, or to ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, and similar laws)

We do not sell consumer health data and do not share it for advertising. Information you choose to send us about your health is used only to respond to you, and you may ask us to delete it at any time.

International visitors

Bayit Miklot is based in the United States, and information is processed in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • We rely on your consent for advertising cookies, our legitimate interests to respond to your messages and run the Site, and our legal obligations to keep donation records.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to or restrict its use, request a copy of it, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. Payment data is handled by PCI-compliant processors. We keep donation records as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, and other information only as long as needed.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed, and we will note material changes here.

Refund Policy

We are grateful for every gift, and we want giving to be simple and trustworthy.

Donations are generally final

Donations are charitable gifts, and they are generally non-refundable once processed. Donations are put to work quickly to support our programs, and your receipt may be used for tax purposes.

When we will refund a donation

We will refund a donation made in error. Examples include:

  • a duplicate donation,
  • an incorrect amount (for example, an extra zero), or
  • a charge you did not authorize.

How to request a refund

Email {{ org.email }} within 30 days of the donation. Include your name, the date and amount of the donation, the last four digits of the card used, and the reason for the request.

We will reply within five (5) business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank may take five to ten (5–10) business days to show the credit. When a donation is refunded, its tax receipt is no longer valid.

Requests made more than 30 days after a donation are reviewed case by case.

Gifts through The Donors’ Fund or donor-advised funds

Grants from a donor-advised fund, including The Donors’ Fund, cannot be refunded to an individual donor. Please contact your fund sponsor about any error, and we will work with the sponsor to correct it.

Disputed charges

If you do not recognize a charge from Bayit Miklot, please contact us before disputing it with your bank. We can usually resolve the issue faster.

Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how to cancel donations made to Bayit Miklot through this website.

One-time donations

A one-time donation is processed immediately, so it cannot be canceled once it is complete. If you made a mistake, please see our Refund Policy.

Recurring donations

If you set up a recurring (for example, monthly) donation, you may cancel it at any time. To cancel:

  • use the link to manage your donation in your email receipt, if one is provided, or
  • email {{ org.email }} or call {{ org.phone_display }} with your name, the email address used for the donation, and the amount.

Please contact us at least three (3) business days before your next scheduled payment. We will confirm the cancellation by email. A cancellation stops future payments. It does not refund payments already made.

Gifts through The Donors’ Fund or donor-advised funds

Grants and recurring grants made through The Donors’ Fund or another donor-advised fund are controlled by that sponsoring organization. Please contact your fund sponsor to cancel or change them.

Accessibility Statement

Bayit Miklot is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we do

  • Text and buttons are designed to meet WCAG color-contrast requirements, in both light and dark mode.
  • The site can be navigated with a keyboard, includes a “Skip to content” link, and shows a visible focus outline.
  • Images include text descriptions, and decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • The site respects reduced-motion and high-contrast settings, and works when zoomed or viewed on small screens.

Need help or found a problem?

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or need information in a different format, please email {{ org.email }} or call {{ org.phone_display }}. Tell us the page and the problem, and we will respond within five (5) business days and provide the information another way if needed.
