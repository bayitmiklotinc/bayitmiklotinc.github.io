Privacy Policy

Bayit Miklot Inc. (“Bayit Miklot,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through bayitmiklot.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email or call us, we receive your name, contact details and anything you choose to share.
  • Donation information. Card donations are processed by a third party processor. The third party collects your payment details and shares with us your name, email, billing details, donation amount and date. We never see or store your full card number. Gifts through The Donors’ Fund or other donor-advised funds are handled by those organizations, which may share your name and gift details with us.
  • Automatic information. The Site uses the Google tag (Google Ads) to measure visits and the effectiveness of our outreach. Google may set cookies and collect information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring site.

Health information

Please do not send detailed medical or mental health information by email or through the Site. Email is not a secure channel. If you are seeking help, share only your contact information, and we will arrange a confidential conversation.

How we use information

  • to process donations and send receipts,
  • to respond to inquiries and requests for help,
  • to thank donors and share news about our work (you may opt out at any time),
  • to understand and improve the Site and our outreach, and
  • to meet legal, accounting and tax obligations.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us operate (for example a
  • third-party processor for donations and payments, Google for
  • analytics and advertising, and GitHub for website hosting),
  • when required by law or to protect the safety of any person, or
  • with your consent.

Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can limit personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

To unsubscribe from our emails, or to ask us to access, correct or delete your information, contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. Payment data is handled by PCI-compliant processors. We keep donation records as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, and other information only as long as needed.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

Refund Policy

We are grateful for every gift, and we want giving to be simple and trustworthy.

Donations are generally final

Donations are charitable gifts, and they are generally non-refundable once processed. Donations are put to work quickly to support our programs, and your receipt may be used for tax purposes.

When we will refund a donation

We will refund a donation made in error. Examples include:

  • a duplicate donation,
  • an incorrect amount (for example, an extra zero), or
  • a charge you did not authorize.

How to request a refund

Email info@bayitmiklot.com within 30 days of the donation. Include your name, the date and amount of the donation, the last four digits of the card used, and the reason for the request.

We will reply within five (5) business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank may take five to ten (5–10) business days to show the credit. When a donation is refunded, its tax receipt is no longer valid.

Requests made more than 30 days after a donation are reviewed case by case.

Gifts through The Donors’ Fund or donor-advised funds

Grants from a donor-advised fund, including The Donors’ Fund, cannot be refunded to an individual donor. Please contact your fund sponsor about any error. We will work with the sponsor to correct it.

Disputed charges

If you do not recognize a charge from Bayit Miklot, please contact us before disputing it with your bank. We can usually resolve the issue faster.

Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how to cancel donations made to Bayit Miklot Inc. (“Bayit Miklot”) through this website.

One-time donations

A one-time donation is processed immediately, so it cannot be cancelled once it is complete. If you made a mistake, please see our Refund Policy.

Recurring donations

If you set up a recurring (for example, monthly) donation, you may cancel it at any time. To cancel:

  • use the link to manage your donation in your email receipt, if one is provided, or
  • email avi@bayitmiklot.com or call 917-676-2014 with your name, the email used for the donation, and the amount.

Please contact us at least three (3) business days before your next scheduled payment. We will confirm the cancellation by email. A cancellation stops future payments. It does not refund payments already made.

Gifts through The Donors’ Fund or donor-advised funds

Grants and recurring grants made through The Donors’ Fund or another donor-advised fund are controlled by that sponsoring organization. Please contact your fund sponsor to cancel or change them.
